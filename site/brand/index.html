§ 01

Foundations

TRIVIU makes technical systems legible, testable and verifiable. The identity does not sell a result: it shows the route, the limits, the costs and the choices.

“The brand never arrives finished. It is becoming.”

Principles of expression

Non-custody

Communicate that the user keeps control, and name the limits plainly.

Open source

Contracts, parameters, blocks and tests are first-class elements, not footnotes.

No promises

Never use guarantees, projections or captures of supposed gains.

Editorial rule

Education comes before execution. Any interface touching capital, risk or a transaction must show context, source and the nature of the data. Synthetic content carries a seal — and the seal never hides, not even when the screen is narrow.

§ 02

Origin of the name

TRIVIU comes from trivium, Latin for the meeting of three ways. The name also carries the classical route of learning that turns information into understanding.

The trivium

Three roads that meet. The brand organises complexity without erasing the relations inside it.

The route

A path built while advancing. The stroke is not a finished outline: it reveals the route.

The trilemma

Three forces that must be made explicit. No technical decision is presented as magic or free of trade-off.

§ 03

The mark — Triangle Motion

The logo is a triangle that draws itself: three vertices, an open lower corner, a dotted trace and a final point that arrives last. The motion is not decoration; it is the idea of continuous construction.

Primary · ink on paper · 16.98:1

Dark application · same geometry, same timing · 15.55:1

Canonical geometry

viewBox: 0 0 320 296 path: M75 168 L160 20 L245 168 L104 168 nodes: (75,168) r9 open ring · (160,20) r9 · (245,168) r9 · (104,168) r4.5 base: stroke-dasharray 2 11 · opacity .24 flow: stroke-dasharray 68 390 · stroke-dashoffset −458 · 5,200 ms linear
The fourth node is not optional

The open corner and the final point at (104,168) are identity, not detail. The institutional site shipped the mark seven times and dropped the fourth node in all seven; the Console carried it in nine of nine. A mark without its last point is a different mark.

What never changes

✓ Preserve

Original proportion, the open corner, the dotted trace, the final point, and the direction of drawing: origin → apex → right → opening.

✕ Avoid

Closing the base, turning it into a solid triangle, applying a gradient, distorting it, rotating it, or baking a background colour into the SVG.

§ 04

Motion — flow, never a jump

The triangle moves by the act of being drawn. One cycle is 5,200 ms, linear and repeatable. The trace travels the path without bouncing, spinning or switching between prepared shapes, and the nodes stay still: they do not pop.

The dotted base is the route that exists before it is walked. The travelling segment is the walking.

Permitted states

The three permitted states of the mark
StateUseBehaviour
motionHero, onboarding, reveal, sizes ≥ 48 px Draws continuously in 5,200 ms and restarts without a flash.
staticSignature sizes (< 48 px), documents, print, fallback Complete path, all four nodes, no animation.
reducedprefers-reduced-motion Complete composition, no animation. The dash array must be reset — see below.
Stopping the animation is not enough

Setting animation:none while leaving stroke-dasharray:68 390 in place leaves the mark as a loose 68-unit fragment, not a triangle. The reduced-motion rule must also reset the dash array. The institutional site shipped exactly this defect.

Optical compensation — ratified

The canonical stroke width of 5 measures 5/320 of the width. In a 32 px signature that is 0.5 px — sub-pixel. That is the mechanical reason hand-redrawn marks appeared with stroke 14 in the brand kit and 18 in a thumbnail: someone was fixing thinness in the wrong place.

Below 48 px the mark uses the static state with a heavier optical stroke. Proportion, path, node positions and drawing direction stay untouched; only the stroke weight changes, and only at the declared sizes.

§ 05

The points of the triangle

Each point is one axis of the distributed-systems trilemma. The triangle stays open because the brand promises no perfect solution: it shows that every architecture picks priorities and declares its limits.

Top point Decentralisation

Distribute control, reduce dependence on a single authority, and make the rules verifiable by more than one party.

D · distributed control

Right point Security

Preserve integrity, ownership, validation, and the ability to stop or reject an unsafe operation.

S · verifiable integrity

Open point Scalability

Allow growth in use and volume without hiding cost, latency, congestion or lost guarantees.

E · capacity with limits

The open point is not a drawing flaw. It is the visual reminder that the third axis has to be chosen, measured and documented.

§ 06

Colour with function

Verified is ratified here. Brandbook v3.3 defined no colour for a verified state. Design System v4.2 already carried #237A3B. It was promoted, not invented, and this document ratifies it as a primitive.

The dark tier — why it exists

The Brandbook palette was designed for paper. Measured against the product's dark surface it fails: Ultramar, the brand's action colour, reads at 2.36:1 on #131519. That is the mechanical reason three surfaces drifted to an off-brand acid green, which measures 15.17:1 there. The fix was not to force the primitive back — it was to add the tier that was missing.

The tier is derived against panel-2 #242830, the lightest surface text can land on. Aiming at the base background instead passed there and failed on panels by a tenth.

Two roles, two tokens

The dark variant is text on a dark surface. Used as a fill, white on it reads 3.11:1 and fails. Filled surfaces use the primitive, where white reads 7.74:1. --action and --action-fill are different tokens because they answer different questions.

Focus ring

No single shade of Ultramar clears 3:1 on paper and on the dark surface: the primitive gives 7.40 on paper and 2.36 on night; the dark variant inverts it. The focus ring is therefore surface-dependent, like every other semantic role.

Compliance, measured

Fourteen text pairs, measured in the browser rather than asserted: 14/14 pass WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1) · 5/14 reach AAA (7:1). AA is the declared target for this system. Reaching AAA would require changing brand primitives — Lacre and Violeta are 1.5–1.9 short — and that is a brand decision, not a styling one. It is recorded here as open, not quietly skipped.

§ 07

Typography

Three families, three jobs: human narrative, legible interface, precise data.

Display · Libre Caslon Text

The brand is under construction.

Covers, editorial headings and positioning.

Interface · Public Sans

The interface reports state, limit and next step without manufacturing urgency.

Body, navigation, buttons and forms.

Data · IBM Plex Mono

triangle.draw · 5200ms · 0x8f3C…a91D · synthetic data

Hashes, blocks, parameters, metrics and logs.

Fonts are self-hosted, never fetched

Until the three families are bundled as local woff2, each stack falls back to a system face with close metrics. No surface may pull a font from an external host: a brand that stops rendering when a CDN blinks is not a brand asset.

§ 09

Components

Components are not decoration: each one states a condition, a piece of evidence or a permitted action.

Seals

Verified Stated Pending Spec AI-generated Illustrative

Stated — in the reference material. Pending — awaiting evidence. Spec — confirm against the contract. Illustrative — synthetic, never a record of anything that happened. A number without a seal is a claim.

Actions

Risk notice

Risk notice

Gas, slippage, liquidity and competition change results. Without sufficient evidence the interface keeps the state unknown rather than guessing in the user's favour.

Component model

Route card

Discovery → quote → fence. Shows the stage without implying it is executable.

Evidence table

Claim → source → status. Every number carries an origin, a moment and a verification state.

Trade-off record

Gain · hold · cost. Chain decisions are documented with what was paid for them.

§ 10

Taxonomy

The same visual construction takes different jobs by context. The taxonomy separates what is identity, state, evidence and risk.

System taxonomy
LayerWhat it represents Where it applies
BrandTriangle Motion Logo, signature, onboarding, documentation.
AxesDecentralisation · Security · Scalability Trilemma, trade-offs, diagrams, architecture decisions.
StatesMotion · Static · Reduced Continuous flow, documentary fallback, accessibility.
EvidenceContract · block · test · source Data, metrics, audit and technical claims.
RiskCost · gas · slippage · revert Any screen touching execution or capital.

Colour, type and motion signal the layer; they do not replace evidence. The mark identifies the system — it never validates a result on its own.

§ 11

Governance

Consistency protects the experience. The system guards the brand against promises, custody confusion and falsely positive states.

Do

Show the limit. Give status, source, timestamp, unit, cost and the reason for a rejection.

Do not

Dress uncertainty in green. Never invent a metric, never write “audited” without an audit, and never call a console a vault when there is no custody.

Publication checklist

  • Does the mark keep its open lower corner and its fourth node?
  • Are the dotted trace and the points in the original proportion?
  • Does motion honour 5,200 ms, and does reduced motion reset the dash array rather than only stopping the animation?
  • Is every synthetic or AI-generated figure sealed — including on a 320 px screen?
  • Is there a risk notice wherever the screen touches execution?
  • Does every technical claim carry a contract, block, PR or verifiable source?
  • Does the brand name carry translate="no"? Automatic translation renders TRIVIU as “TRIVIA”.
