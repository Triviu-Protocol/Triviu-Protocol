01 · origin of the mark

Three roads.
One open path.

TRIVIU comes from trivium: the meeting of three ways. The mark draws that origin as a triangle that is alive, continuous and deliberately unfinished.

The mark never stops learning.

Motion runs from the origin to the apex, reaches the third point and returns along the base without closing the shape. That opening is the argument: distributed systems keep evolving through choices, evidence and new context.

  • D Decentralisation
    Legible rules and distributed control.
  • S Security
    Validation, integrity and explicit conditions.
  • E Scalability
    Room to grow while naming costs and limits.

Limits written before the action.

Build · publish · earn

Write a strategy. Let the network use it.

Compose a mandate in the builder — network, assets, cooldown, allocation, slippage, gas, minimum profit — publish it to the Commons, and every vault that adopts it pays you a share of its own half. You never touch anyone's funds to earn.

planned The protocol's half is untouchable. The author's share is carved out of the user's half only, at a rate the user sees before adopting — and it only ever applies to a positive eligible result. A losing operation pays nobody. No published contract implements this split today; it is the specification the contracts must meet, not a description of code that runs.

11 · frequently asked

Clarity before
the next action.

Straight answers, including the ones that say no.

What is TRIVIU?

A proposal for organising strategies, vaults, multichain context and collaboration in one DeFi ecosystem. This build presents the product and its interfaces.

Do I need a wallet to look around?

No. The site, Labs and the demo Console can be explored with no wallet, no signature and no deposit.

What changes between Essential and Professional?

Essential leads with the overview and common actions. Professional adds parameters, routes, micro-operations and diagnostics. The mode never changes permissions.

Does following a strategy start an operation?

No. Following authors and watching strategies are social acts of observation. Any execution flow needs its own authorisation and validations.

Are the three chains connected already?

No. In this build the selectors present visual contexts. Real integration needs a source, freshness, error handling and per-chain data isolation.

Is the 50/50 split running on-chain today?

No. The split is the specification the contracts must meet. No published contract implements it yet, which is why every economic figure on this page carries a planned or illustrative seal.

Do the numbers shown prove returns?

No. Synthetic data exists to judge the experience. Performance has to be demonstrated with verifiable sources, costs and an identified period. Nothing here promises a return, and past results would not guarantee future ones.
